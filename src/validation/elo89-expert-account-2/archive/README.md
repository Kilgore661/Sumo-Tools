# Expert Account Drafting Archive

This directory preserves documents produced while revising the Elo-89 expert
account. They are retained for audit and historical purposes but are not part
of the current public account and must not be used as its specification.

## Contents

- **Elo-89 Development and Decision Narrative** preserves the former Chapter
  10 and a compact history of model development. It includes superseded names,
  interpretations and implementation descriptions.
- **Expert Account Review - State of Play** records an intermediate review.
  Many of its identified issues were resolved by later edits and
  investigations.
- **Revision Provenance** records how the 30 October 2026 revision related to
  the preceding expert account.
- **Working Sources** preserves the uncurated chapter-by-chapter source lists
  used during drafting. The public appendix now provides the curated guide to
  current supporting work.

The development narrative and working-source list contain paths recorded
relative to the parent `elo89-expert-account-2` directory, their original
location. Moving them here did not convert those historical path lists into
current-model references.

For the current account, begin with [`../README.md`](../README.md) and
[`../00 Contents.md`](../00%20Contents.md).
