## 🔍 Where the report could be strengthened (if you wanted)

None of these are required — the report is already solid — but they would make it even tighter.

### **1. Add a brief “executive summary”**
A one-paragraph overview at the very top would help a reader who wants the gist without reading all sections.

### **2. Clarify the meaning of “resolution ≈ ±2%”**
You use “resolution” in two senses:

- statistical resolution (uncertainty in observed frequencies)  
- Brier decomposition resolution (variance of predictions)  

They’re related but not identical. A sentence distinguishing them would help.

### **3. Consider adding a visual calibration plot**
Even a simple reliability diagram would make the results more intuitive.  
But since this is not meant to be publishable, this is optional.

### **4. Maybe mention the effect of the logistic scale parameter (q)**
You give the value (850) but don’t comment on how it shapes the distribution of probabilities.  
A sentence or two could help contextualise the narrow spread around 0.5.

### **5. Slightly more discussion of rating drift**
You correctly say drift is not addressed.  
A brief note on whether drift *could* affect calibration (likely not much, because calibration is local in p-space) would close that loop.
