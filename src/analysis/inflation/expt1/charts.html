Experiment 1: unnormalised rating turnover

Fixed P1 · divisional K · no mean restoration · ratings preserved across gaps. The mean covers the represented population, not a fixed rank. Turnover or a rising mean alone does not establish inflation at upper chii.

Points entering and leaving

Removed points are drawn below zero. First-basho allocations establish initial stock and are excluded from all flow charts. Returns restore their saved rating; they are not new allocations.

Population mean

Pre-basho means follow membership changes and precede bouts. No smoothing or burn-in is applied.

Final-departure donor balance

Entry allocation minus departure rating: positive for donors, negative for those leaving above entry. Temporary gaps are excluded. Final departure is inferred within the selected replay history; it is not verified retirement. The initial cohort has observation-start allocations, not known career-entry ratings.

Temporary gaps and bout mass

Gap inflows and outflows affect represented membership but do not allocate new points. Bout mass is the sum of actual participant updates and reconciles the effect of unequal K.
