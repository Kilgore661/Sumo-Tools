Notes

  1. In Combined view, Row is a displayed row number. It changes when the table is sorted or filtered.
  2. The reported Shikona and Chii are those held by the rikishi at the end of the reporting period. (E.g. a rikishi promoted in the final basho is shown at the higher rank.)
  3. Wins include fusensho.
  4. Average is Wins divided by the selected number of basho.
  5. # values are competition ranking positions computed over the currently displayed rows.
  6. An Active rikishi is one that is listed on the banzuke for the latest basho.
  7. Bouts is the number of bouts the rikishi was expected to fight over the selected basho, taking account of the division they were in at the time. (E.g. 5 basho in sekitori and one in Makushita means 5×15 + 7 = 82 bouts.)
  8. Win % is Wins divided by Bouts.
