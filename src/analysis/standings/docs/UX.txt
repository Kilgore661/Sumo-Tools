! Contextual note? Eg in Advanced mode, you can set the number of basho to much larger numbers; in this case, showing inactive (retired) rikishi will lead to ghost rikishi being displayed.

Advanced Mode - shows number of basho, division, active rikishi switch

New "number of basho Note": It is unlikely that you want to set the number of
basho to more than 6 and have Active rikishi off because you will see "ghost
rikishi" i.e. rikishi who have retired and so arguably not relevant. Eg with
60 basho, Hakuho is still in the top 40 even though he has not competed for 5
years.

New division Note. The rikishi making up any given division changes from basho
to basho. The extent of the "churn" varies from division to division,
generally increasing from Maukkuchi (~10% the division change) to Jonokuchi
(~50%). Selecting a division below juryo is probably not useful.
