# Experiment 1: Validation of invariants and mass behaviour in (M_{\mathrm{Elo}})

## 1. Objective

To verify that the implementation of (M_{\mathrm{Elo}}):

1. satisfies its theoretical invariants, and
2. exhibits expected behaviour with respect to rating mass under entry and exit.

---

## 2. Scope

This experiment concerns:

* a **single pass** of (M_{\mathrm{Elo}}) over a historical dataset,
* with a specified policy for handling exit (as discussed in *Matters Arising*),
* and initialization at (b).

It does **not** concern:

* fixed-point iteration,
* parameter optimisation,
* or interpretability of ratings beyond basic sanity.

---

## 3. Inputs

The experiment requires:

* A history:

  * ordered sequence of bouts (b : \mathcal{T} \to \mathcal{I} \times \mathcal{I} \times O)
  * chii function (c : \mathcal{I} \times \mathcal{T} \to C)

* Model parameters:

  * (b \in B)
  * (q \in Q)
  * (k : C \to K)

* A policy for exit:

  * e.g. redistribution of deviation from mean, or persistence

---

## 4. Outputs

The implementation must produce:

* rating states (r_t) for all (t \in \mathcal{T})

and additionally expose:

* total rating:
  [
  S_t = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_t} r_t(i)
  ]

* mean rating:
  [
  \mu_t = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{I}_t|} S_t
  ]

* per-event changes:

  * change in total rating per bout
  * change in total rating at entry
  * change in total rating at exit

---

## 5. Requirements: Invariants

The following must hold (up to floating-point tolerance):

### 5.1 Bout conservation

For every bout (t):

[
S_t = S_{t^-}
]

Equivalently:

* the sum of ratings is unchanged by a bout.

---

### 5.2 Mean invariance under fixed population

If (\mathcal{I}*t = \mathcal{I}*{t^-}), then:

[
\mu_t = \mu_{t^-}
]

---

### 5.3 Entry behaviour

When a rikishi joins at time (t):

* their initial rating is (b),
* the new mean satisfies:

[
\mu_t = \mu_{t^-}
]

provided (\mu_{t^-} = b).

---

### 5.4 Exit behaviour (if redistribution policy is used)

When a rikishi leaves:

* the redistribution must ensure:

[
\mu_t = \mu_{t^-}
]

---

### 5.5 Global invariant

If exit is handled by redistribution and entry is at (b), then:

[
\mu_t = b \quad \forall t
]

---

## 6. Requirements: Scale of exit adjustments

Let a rikishi leaving at time (t) have rating (r(i)), and let:

[
\delta = r(i) - \mu_{t^-}
]

Let (n = |\mathcal{I}_{t^-}| - 1) be the size of the remaining population.

Then the per-rikishi adjustment is:

[
\frac{\delta}{n}
]

The implementation must compute and record:

* (\delta) for each exit
* (n)
* (|\delta/n|)

---

### 6.1 Required statistics

The experiment must report:

* distribution of (|\delta/n|)
* maximum (|\delta/n|)
* typical (e.g. median) (|\delta/n|)

---

### 6.2 Comparative scale

For context, also record:

* typical magnitude of a single-bout Elo update

This allows comparison:

[
\frac{|\delta/n|}{\text{typical bout update}}
]

---

## 7. Evaluation criteria

The experiment should allow the following conclusions:

1. **Correctness**

   * All invariants hold to numerical tolerance

2. **Mass behaviour**

   * Mean remains equal to (b) throughout

3. **Negligibility of redistribution**

   * Exit adjustments are small relative to typical rating changes

---

## 8. Relation to “Matters Arising”

This experiment specifically addresses:

* conservation of rating mass
* effect of entry and exit
* significance of redistribution policy

It provides empirical evidence for whether these belong:

* in the core model, or
* as commentary-level considerations

---

## 9. Non-requirements

This experiment does not require:

* correctness of chii ordering
* realism of ratings
* convergence or stability over time
* fixed-point behaviour

---

## Summary

> Implement (M_{\mathrm{Elo}}) for a single pass and verify that rating mass is conserved under bout updates, preserved under entry and compensated exit, and that redistribution adjustments are negligible in scale.
