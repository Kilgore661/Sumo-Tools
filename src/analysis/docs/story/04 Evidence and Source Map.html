Evidence and Source Map

Detailed inventory

The initial repository review is recorded in Elo Documentation Rummage. It identifies the Markdown and HTML material about Elo, Equelo and the related experiments.

This note is the shorter map for constructing the eventual account.

Current public-facing explanations

  • Elo Ratings is the clearest existing introduction to ordinary Elo and its immediate historical sumo problems.
  • Equelo Ratings is existing site prose and should be reviewed against the structure in this folder.
  • Equelo Assumptions records assumptions currently exposed to readers.
  • What Is an Equelo Rating? is the project-level account of interpretation and limits.

Basic Elo predictive evidence

  • Prediction README maps the chronological Basic Elo experiments.
  • Findings consolidates the measured results, population differences and initialisation experiments.
  • A Defence of Elo for Sumo (Sketch) gives the narrowest reader-facing claim currently supported.

These sources support a modest aggregate predictive claim. They do not establish true strength, universal parameter choices or predictive validity for Equelo.

Probability and calibration

  • Probability README maps the probability experiments.
  • Results and Findings records the current calibration results and their supported regions.
  • Using Fixed Point Initialisation is relevant to the predictive effect of Equelo-like entrant ratings.

Elo behaviour and incomplete comparisons

  • Toy Elo README maps the controlled comparison-graph experiments.
  • What Ratings Might Say About Grand Sumo supplies the most important interpretive guardrails.
  • Boundary Monotonicity tests a scheduling explanation for the lower-Makuuchi anomaly.
  • Elo Bottom Line 2 explains continuing fixed-k fluctuation and the limits of the available theoretical bound.

Equelo mechanics and unresolved behaviour

  • Equelo README identifies the current production model and access contract.
  • Fixed Supported Requirements and Fixed Supported Design define the maintained construction.
  • Initial Rating Audit records support and completion concerns.
  • Lower-Rank Problems gives the clearest account of churn, sparse support and pathological tail behaviour.

These documents describe current production and the failure that motivated its support policy. They do not define the adopted next entrant-prior policy.

Contextual initial-rating policy

  • The M12 Problem: Consolidated Research Record records the chronology, evidence, interpretation and decision that closed the M12 issue as a documentation blocker.
  • M12 Experiment Catalogue records the commands, matched artifacts and headline results for the literal, boundary, dual-boundary and lower-banzuke experiments.
  • Initial Rating Policy is the normative decision for the adopted 1989-onward paired entrant priors.
  • Fixed-Boundary Equelo defines the contextual Makuuchi--Juryo source experiment.
  • Continuous Lower-Banzuke Equelo defines the second contextual source experiment.
  • Initial-Rating Reconciliation defines the persisted merge and pairing producers.

The policy is adopted but not yet the map used by current production Equelo. Its source fixed points are experimental evidence; the final reconciliation is a declared construction rather than the direct fixed point of one simulation.

Clean Elo and chii disagreement

  • Clean Elo README records the negative result and the limits of interpretation.
  • Rating Probe Findings contains the detailed monotonicity and boundary-alignment findings.

These sources are central to explaining why neither agreement nor disagreement between a rating system and chii should be treated as self-interpreting.

Historical material

The older dated planning notes and archived site prose are useful for tracing the development of the ideas, but should not be used as authority where newer specifications or findings exist. The detailed rummage document identifies those files and their current evidential status.
