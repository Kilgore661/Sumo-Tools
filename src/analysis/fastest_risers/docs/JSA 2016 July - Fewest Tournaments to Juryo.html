Provenance

Transcribed from the Japan Sumo Association’s 2016 July Grand Sumo Tournament Banzuke Topics, page 2, Table 4: “Fewest Tournament to Juryo Debut (Pro Debut After 1958 - Excluding Makushita Special Debuts)”. The source is the project’s local copy of betsuhyo201607en.pdf.

This is a historical snapshot published in July 2016, not an updated record list. Names, dates, counts, tied positions and parentheses are retained as printed. The position numbers have been separated from the rikishi names, and merged tournament-count cells have been repeated on each row for readability. No values have been recalculated or corrected.
