This is a public site for Grand Sumo results, rankings, careers, records, and ratings. It is meant to work at more than one level: you can open a page and read the default view, or use the Options to ask more specific questions.

Start with GOATs, Basho Results or Most Recent Banzuke and click on/hover over stuff.

Some pages are plain historical records; some add experimental ratings or analysis. The ratings are tools for comparison, not official judgements. If you just want a feel for the numbers, see Equelo vs Chii. If you want the reasoning behind them, start with Why Ratings?.

What this site is not

This site is not an official sumo source, a ranking authority, or a claim to have found the true strength of every rikishi. Official ranks are the banzuke; the ratings here are exploratory tools for comparison.

It is also not a survey of rating theory or a comparison with any other possible rating method. The aim is narrower: to describe the tools I built, show the results they produce, and make the assumptions clear enough that the outputs can be read honestly.

All the sumo data used here comes from the very wonderful people at SumoDB, to whom I am much obliged. Any errors in how I downloaded the data, processed, interpreted, or present them are mine.
