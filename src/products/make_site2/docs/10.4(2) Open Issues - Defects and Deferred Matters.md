### Options and Checkbox Grammar

Deferred / TBD.

The site frequently needs to let readers choose one value from a finite set.
The current model supports single finite choices, usually rendered as radio
buttons when the list is short and as a dropdown when the list is longer:

```text
Choose
--------
  Option 1
  Option 2
```

A checkbox is a compact alternative when there are only two choices:

```text
Use Option 1 [yes/no]
```

This saves space and matches existing site style, but it changes the grammar of
the label. A checkbox label must carry both the dimension and the positive
state, which can lead to wider or less consistent labels. For example, Records
pages may want compact population controls such as `Current rikishi` or
`Former rikishi` rather than full questions such as `Show former rikishi?`.

Review the site for consistency. Consider adopting a site-wide policy for when
to render a binary Filter as a checkbox and when to render the same logical
choice as a finite-choice control. The review should cover public wording,
label width, URL-state vocabulary, and consistency across Records pages.

Current Records-control direction:

- Use `Current` and `Former` as the public population vocabulary rather than
  `retired`, `non-retired`, `active` or `inactive`.
- For Records pages where current/former population selection matters, use a
  compact checkbox group:

  ```text
  Rikishi
  [x] Current
  [x] Former
  ```

- Both boxes checked is the default and means the combined all-rikishi
  population. This should apply consistently to Most Consecutive Bouts, Most
  Career Wins, Most Career Losses, Highest Equelo and Longest Careers.
- For Most Consecutive Bouts, replace the current `Clean only?` wording with
  `Whole career`, and remove the visible active/clean-status column if the
  Filter makes the distinction the selected record population rather than a
  displayed fact.
- For Most Career Wins, replace `Count fusen results?` with `Fusensho`, false
  by default.
- For Most Career Losses, replace `Count fusen results?` with `Fusenpai`, false
  by default.
- The ranking semantics of population-specific Records views are deliberately
  deferred. Decide separately whether `#` should be rank within the selected
  population, rank in the all-rikishi population after filtering, or another
  explicitly modelled record-position concept.
