Elo89 account: matters arising

A place to retain ideas, observations and questions that arise while writing the Elo89 account but are peripheral to its immediate purpose. Started 11 September 2026.

Entries are invitations to possible later investigation, not additions to the writing requirements or conditions for completing the account. Record observations separately from conjectures, preserve useful sources, and leave questions open where the evidence is incomplete. New entries can be added as the discussion develops.

1. What official documentation of banzuke decisions exists?

Status: question retained for possible investigation; no general survey undertaken.

The author had understood the determination of chii to be largely undocumented and discretionary. Finding explicit statements on the JSA website suggests that more official documentation may exist than he had realised. This is an opportunity to challenge that understanding rather than assume it is correct.

Two sources were consulted during the writing discussion:

  • JSA mission and organisation: describes maintaining and developing sumo's traditions and order, and promoting sumo culture.
  • JSA explanation of yokozuna promotion: states criteria including outstanding dignity/character and competitive ability, and two consecutive championships as an ozeki or equivalent strong results.

These establish that some relevant statements are public. They do not amount to a survey of the available rules, establish a complete procedure for allocating banzuke positions, or explain the reasons for a particular decision. The yokozuna criterion should not be generalized into a claim that dignity is assessed when deciding every ordinary rank placement.

Questions worth retaining:

  • What other official rules, regulations, committee procedures or explanatory documents are publicly available, especially in Japanese?
  • Which statements govern banzuke compilation generally, and which concern specific promotions or ranks?
  • Where do published provisions leave discretion through terms such as “normally” or “equivalent”, and are those terms explained elsewhere?
  • What is documented about the number of places at each rank? In particular, is the familiar east/west maegashira structure prescribed explicitly, and how has its historical treatment varied? The author's observation that the structure has not always been the same should be investigated against dated evidence.
  • Which apparent rules are written requirements, which are observed conventions, and which are interpretations by fans or commentators?
  • What evidence, if any, supports the author's impression that public accountability has changed over time?

Bias and interpretation

The author recognises a sceptical prior about the JSA's discretion and possible institutional politics, and wants opportunities to test that prior against evidence. No evidence of improper influence in a particular ranking decision was established in this discussion. Speculation about personal connections or leadership politics remains speculation.

The writing should guard both against importing that suspicion as fact and against supplying an unsupported favourable explanation for decisions. Institutional statements about values tell us what is professed; they do not by themselves establish the reasoning behind each decision. Lack of a rule found so far does not establish that no rule exists.

Relationship to the Elo89 account

The current draft of section 2.1 remains acceptable to the author. This side discussion is not a request to replace it with an investigation of JSA governance or to make reproducibility the sole point of comparison. The main task remains a clear account of Elo89, including its usefulness as a complementary tool alongside chii.

If later investigation produces a finding relevant to that explanation, incorporate it proportionately. Otherwise retain it here for its own interest.
