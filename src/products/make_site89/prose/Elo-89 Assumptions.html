Scope and evidence

Elo-89 is defined for the represented record from January 1989 onward. It assumes that the available binary win/loss results form a sufficiently complete and connected account for an Elo-style model. Fusen and non-binary outcomes are not rated.

Entrant ratings

A new or returning rikishi receives a rating from the fixed P1 chii map. P1 uses banzuke rank as informed starting evidence; it is not a claim that every rikishi at a rank has the same ability.

Population and scale

The active population is shifted uniformly to a fixed mean before and after each basho. This controls movement of the absolute scale caused by entry, departure and unequal divisional K values without changing differences among active ratings when the shift is applied.

Interpretation

The banzuke and an Elo rating answer different questions. The banzuke embeds institutional rules and promotion decisions; Elo-89 summarises performance through its stated update and population policies. Disagreement between them therefore requires interpretation rather than automatic correction of either.
