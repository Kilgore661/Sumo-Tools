Chii is a rikishi's official place on a particular banzuke. Elo-89 is a numerical account of represented bout performance from January 1989 onward. They are related because performance affects promotion and because Elo-89 uses chii to initialise entrants, but they are not interchangeable.

Rikishi at the same chii can have different ratings because their opponents, results and prior histories differ. Conversely, similar ratings do not require identical banzuke positions: promotion rules, available rank slots and the timing of banzuke preparation also matter.

The site's rank and rating views are intended to make those agreements and disagreements visible. Neither view should be treated as a hidden conversion table for the other.
